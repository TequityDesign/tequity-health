What we understand

A healthcare product is more than an application.

Healthcare products must work across patients, care teams, operational workflows, external systems and sensitive data. Adding AI makes the product more powerful, but also introduces new questions around reliability, human oversight and failure handling.

Tequity brings the product, engineering, healthcare integration and AI capabilities needed to make these parts work together.

Applied healthcare AI

AI around real healthcare workflows.

AI makes a healthcare product more capable. It also raises new questions: what happens when it is unsure, who reviews it and how you know it is still working next month. We design for those questions first.

  1. 01
    Built into the workflowAI sits inside the product and its operational systems, not beside them.
  2. 02
    Knows when to involve a humanClear escalation for uncertainty, symptoms and anything outside the agreed policy.
  3. 03
    Evaluated before rolloutTest sets, failure handling and tracing are in place before patients or staff rely on it.
  4. 04
    Practical use casesVoice, documents, workflow and knowledge, where the work is repetitive and the rules are clear.
  5. 05
    Clinical ownership stays with youYour clinical team owns clinical logic and decisions. We build the systems that carry them out.
