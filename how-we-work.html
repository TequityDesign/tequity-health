How we work

From first workflow to a team you own.

We know every founder needs something different, depending on where their product is. So we tailor the engagement to the stage you are in, and change it as you grow.

Not sure which stage you are in? Most founders are between two. That is normal. We will work it out together on the first call. Book a discovery call
Stage 01 · Idea

Prove the workflow, then ship something real.

A healthcare problem and product concept exist, but the workflow and solution still need validation.

Tequity offer

Validate the product, define the workflow, design it and deliver a focused working MVP.

What you need
  • Clarity on the real workflow and who it serves
  • A first version scoped to what matters
  • Something real users can try

Typical first engagement

Workflow discovery and MVP scoping, followed by a focused build. A focused MVP can be shipped in four weeks when the scope, data access and external dependencies allow it.

Expected output
  • Mapped workflow and user journeys
  • Prototype tested with target users
  • Working MVP in production
  • A clear list of what is deliberately out of scope
Stage 02 · Seed

Turn early feedback into a product people rely on.

An early product exists and real users are producing feedback.

Tequity offer

Deploy a flexible product pod to improve workflows, add essential integrations and accelerate adoption.

What you need
  • Faster iteration on the workflows users struggle with
  • The first integrations customers ask for
  • Reliability you can put in front of a clinic

A product and workflow review of what exists, then a prioritised roadmap delivered in short release cycles.

  • Usage instrumentation
  • A steady release cadence
Stage 03 · PMF

More priorities than people. Add owners, not headcount.

Tequity offer

Embed specialist product, engineering, integration, AI and design capability around clearly owned workstreams.

What you need
  • Specialists who can own a workstream end to end
  • Minimal management overhead for your leaders
  • Work that fits your standards and roadmap

One workstream with an agreed owner, outcome and acceptance criteria, such as a new integration, an AI feature or a care-team tool.

Stage 04 · Scale

Sustained capacity, and a path to owning it.

The company needs sustained capacity and stronger ownership of its long-term team.

Tequity offer

What you need
  • A stable team working to your engineering standards
  • Continuity of people and product knowledge
  • An option to bring the team in-house

Team design: roles, standards, security and data-access controls, and a ramp-up plan.

  • A dedicated team operating to your standards
  • Documented processes and ownership
  • Where agreed, a defined route to transfer

About timelines: a blanket four-week promise does not fit every healthcare product. EHR access, clinical validation, regulated devices and multiple external integrations each add time, and we will map them with you before we commit to a date.

The way we work

More than additional capacity.

Tequity's teams work around a defined product outcome, understand the user and workflow, and take responsibility for shipping. That holds whether we are building your MVP or embedding specialists in your team.

01Context before code

We learn the product and the workflow before development starts, so the first sprint builds the right thing.

02Accountable leadership

A named technical and product lead who owns delivery and is the person you call.

03Defined outcomes

Every engagement has outcomes and acceptance criteria you have agreed to, not only hours.

04One team

Designers, QA and engineers working together on the same backlog, not in separate queues.

05Your knowledge, your code

Documentation, handover and knowledge transfer are part of the work. You own what we build.

06A path forward

From an initial project to a dedicated team, and when it makes sense, a team you own.

Scale stage · Selective

Build, operate, then transfer.

When sustained growth requires a dedicated team, Tequity can recruit, establish and operate that team around your product and engineering standards. Once it is stable and you are ready, the team and operating structure can transition to you.

BuildRecruit and set up

Roles, hiring and onboarding to your standards.

OperateRun and stabilise

We manage the team while it delivers on your roadmap.

TransferHand over

People and structure move to you on agreed terms.

Agreed before a Build-Operate-Transfer engagement starts
  • Minimum team size and duration
  • Legal and employment structure
  • Intellectual-property arrangements
  • Transition conditions
  • Pricing and operating responsibility
  • Replacement and retention terms
  • Security and healthcare-data access controls

Offered selectively, when the scale and commitment on both sides justify it.
