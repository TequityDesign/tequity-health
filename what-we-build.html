01Across layers

Healthcare product strategy and design

Healthcare workflows involve more people than most products: patients, caregivers, clinicians, coordinators, billing and operations. We start by mapping who does what, where information changes hands and what the first version must get right, then design and prototype around it.

  • Workflow discoveryInterviews and shadowing with the people who will use it, not only the buyer.
  • Product definition and MVP scopingWhat is in, what is out, and which external dependencies decide the timeline.
  • User journeys and prototypingClickable prototypes tested with patients and care teams before code.
  • Care-team experienceQueues, triage views and handoffs that fit how a shift actually runs.
  • AI interaction designHow the product behaves when AI is uncertain, wrong or needs to hand off.

03Data and integration layer

Healthcare data and integrations

A healthcare product is only as useful as the systems it connects to. We build the interfaces, and we plan for what goes wrong: missing fields, duplicate patients, feeds that arrive late and partners that change their format.

Integration timelines depend on access that sits outside our control, such as EHR vendor approval, partner sandboxes and data-sharing agreements. We identify those dependencies in the first conversation.

  • Exception handling by designRetry and reconciliation queues, mapping errors surfaced to staff, and unmatched records sent for human review.
  • Sandbox to productionTest harnesses and synthetic data so integration work is not blocked on live access.

04Data and integration layer

Data engineering

Once the data arrives, it needs to be cleaned, matched, stored with the right access controls and made useful to the product, the operations team and the AI layer. We build pipelines that are observable and auditable from day one.

  • ETL pipelines and normalisationStandardising inputs such as languages, codes and identifiers across tenants and sources.
  • Multi-tenant data modelsPer-tenant configuration, access and exports without duplicating code.
  • Audit loggingWho saw what, what changed and what the AI decided, kept in one place.
  • Operational analyticsDashboards for throughput, exceptions and AI performance.

05AI layer

Applied healthcare AI

We build AI where it removes real work or waiting: answering the phone, interpreting a conversation, drafting a note, reading a referral or following up with a patient. It is integrated into the product and its operational systems, with a defined path to a person.

  • Voice AITelephony, real-time speech, multilingual calls, call-state handling and timeouts.
  • Agentic workflows and automationMulti-step tasks such as scheduling, follow-ups and form completion, within agreed limits.
  • Document intelligenceReferrals, forms and clinical documents turned into structured data for review.
  • RAG and knowledge systemsAnswers grounded in your policies, protocols and operational knowledge.
  • Evaluation, auditability and escalationTest sets, hallucination and intent checks, tracing of every decision, and human handoff.
What we do not claim Your clinical team owns clinical logic and decisions. We build the systems that carry them out, and the controls that show when they should hand off. We do not provide validated medical algorithms.

06Across layers

QA, security and production readiness

Testing, security and monitoring run alongside delivery from the first sprint. For AI features, that includes evaluation sets and tracing, so you can see what the system did and why.

  • Automated and manual QAIncluding recorded-audio test suites for voice products.
  • Security workAccess controls, 2FA, penetration-test remediation and dependency hygiene.
  • Environments and releaseSeparate UAT and production, controlled releases and rollback.
  • ObservabilityMonitoring, alerting and AI tracing wired into your team's channels.
  • Compliance supportEvidence and remediation for your audits, working with tools such as Vanta.
HIPAA-aware delivery

We design healthcare products around each client's security, privacy and access-control requirements. Where protected health information is involved, the delivery model, infrastructure, data access and contractual responsibilities are agreed before development begins.

A note on compliance There is no universal government HIPAA certification, and compliance depends on the whole operating arrangement, not software alone. We will not tell you a product is compliant by default. We will help you build it so your compliance programme can stand behind it.
