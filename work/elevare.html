Our work/Elevare
Elevare · Maternal health

A maternal-health patient app, rebuilt for the care team behind it

We rebuilt Elevare's maternal-health patient application in React Native, designed it around the care-team context patients rely on and carried out security-readiness work alongside the rebuild. EHR integration work is under way.

About the company

Elevare works in maternal health, supporting patients with an application that keeps them connected to the care team looking after them.

The challenge

  1. 01
    A rebuild, not a reskinThe app needed a new technical foundation the team could keep extending.
  2. 02
    Care-team contextPatients need to see what their care team has asked of them, and why.
  3. 03
    Security readinessThe product needed to be ready for the security reviews healthcare partners run.
  4. 04
    Connecting to clinical systemsInformation needs to move between the app and the care team's systems.

What Tequity built

React Native patient appRebuild
  • New app architecture on React Native
  • Core patient flows rebuilt
  • A release pipeline the team can own
Care-team contextExperience
  • Care-team messages and tasks inside the patient experience
  • Reminders tied to the care plan
Security workReadiness
  • Review of access controls and data handling
  • Remediation ahead of partner security reviews
EHR integrationIn progress
  • Integration scoping and build are under way. We will update this story when it is live.

Process and approach

We treated the rebuild as a chance to design around the care team, not only to change frameworks, and ran security work in parallel so it was not left to the end.
