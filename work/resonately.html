About the company

Resonately helps clinicians spend less time writing notes. A recorded visit becomes a draft note that the clinician reviews, edits and approves.

The challenge

  1. 01
    Capture without frictionRecording had to work in the browser, without extra hardware or software.
  2. 02
    Clinician stays in controlNothing is filed until the clinician has reviewed and approved it.
  3. 03
    Delivery into existing systemsThe note had to arrive where the practice already works: in Sycle.
  4. 04
    Drafts worth reviewingStructured sections that are quick to check and edit.

What Tequity built

RecordingCapture
  • Browser and web recording
  • Upload and processing of recorded visits
Drafting and clinician reviewReview
  • Structured draft notes
  • Editing and approval by the clinician
Sycle integrationDelivery
  • Delivery of approved notes into Sycle
  • Delivery status visible to the practice

Process and approach

The review step was designed first, because it decides whether clinicians trust the product. Recording and delivery were built around it.
