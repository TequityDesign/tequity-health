Outcomes

  • 50,000+voice minutes of real-time medical interpretation a month
  • Edge-case languages supported, including Swahili, Haitian Creole and Azerbaijani
  • Infrastructure built to the client's HIPAA requirements, with compliance audit items closed through Vanta
  • In-app, telephony and dashboard channels running in production

About the company

Standard translation tools struggle in clinical settings. Medical terminology, languages spoken by immigrant communities and the latency of a live consultation all get in the way. The client builds real-time, clinically tuned interpretation for clinics and their patients.

The challenge

  1. 01
    Real-time voiceSub-second end-to-end latency in noisy rooms and on phone lines, including uncommon languages.
  2. 02
    Multilingual callsSpeakers switch language mid-conversation, and the system has to follow.
  3. 03
    Safety of interpretationA wrong translation can harm a patient, so hallucination and intent detection were required.
  4. 04
    Telephony and call stateClean integrations with 3CX, Telnyx and Twilio, plus handling for silence, drop-offs and timeouts.
  5. 05
    Operational visibilityEvery call logged and observable as monthly volume grew.

What Tequity built

Infrastructure and complianceFoundations
  • HIPAA-aware frontend, backend and DevOps pipelines
  • Separate UAT and production environments
  • Observability, alerting and compliance closure with Vanta
Real-time interpretationPipeline
  • Streaming speech-to-text tuned across Soniox and ElevenLabs
  • Hands-free flows through LiveKit and 3CX, with S3 and webhook integration
  • Multi-language diarisation for mid-stream language switching
  • Hallucination detection, intent detection, inactivity-based call termination and auto-timeout
  • Automated audio-file test suites
Interpreter dashboard and escalationOperations
  • Dashboard with analytics and domain-aware feature flags
  • Twilio integration, clinic onboarding and department mapping
  • Escalation paths to human interpreters
Continuous improvementOngoing
  • Slack-based observability for the operations team
  • Department-aware prompts and progressive language additions
  • Volume, audio-worklet and transcription performance tuning

Process and approach

One team owned the stack end to end, from audio capture to telephony to the dashboard. Latency, safety and reliability problems could be fixed where they started, rather than negotiated between vendors.
