About the company

Stitch works at a critical point in patient recovery: the weeks and months after a transplant. The platform combines AI-assisted care coordination with context-aware communication and proactive follow-up, giving care coordinators, surgeons and hospitals a shared view of each patient.

The challenge

  1. 01
    Ship an MVP under time pressureAdmin, coordinator and provider tools had to be usable by real teams quickly.
  2. 02
    Pivot mid-buildThe product refocused on post-transplant patients while development was already under way.
  3. 03
    AI with clear oversightHigh-risk patients meant every AI signal needed a care coordinator in the loop.
  4. 04
    Security expectationsPenetration testing and 2FA were required as the product moved towards hospitals.
  5. 05
    Many user typesCoordinators, surgeons and hospital teams each needed their own view of the same patient.

What Tequity built

MVPPhase 1
  • Designed and built admin, care-coordinator and provider dashboards
  • Patient escalation, updated profiles and Kanban-style triage
  • Internal release and full launch in June 2025, followed by the provider dashboard
Pivot to transplant carePhase 2
  • Reshaped the product for post-transplant patients
  • Modular, configurable care plans for clinic- and condition-specific needs
  • Automated messaging with AI-flagged patient responses
  • Voice calling (v0), media sharing and configurable surgeon and hospital views
AI-first care coordinationPhase 3
  • An AI simulator so coordinators can see how the system responds before patients do
  • A new AI architecture with voice-call note-taking
  • A web-search component to bring in clinical context
  • Caregiver intervention flows
  • Provider experience moved from a surgeon-based to a hospital-based model
  • A common coordinator view released to UAT
Security and qualityThroughout
  • Penetration-test findings addressed and 2FA implemented
  • A continuous backlog for design QC, bug fixes and stability

Process and approach

A stable cross-functional team of product, design, engineering and QA absorbed a strategic pivot without losing delivery pace. The team re-architected mid-build, evolved the AI stack and kept supporting production users throughout. This is the product-partner model we aim for at the Idea and Seed stages.

Where our work ends

What we built

Product strategy, design, engineering and QA for the coordinator, provider and admin tools, and for the patient communication and AI layers.

What this story does not claim

This story does not report clinical outcomes, readmission effects or hospital adoption. Those are Stitch's to measure and share.
