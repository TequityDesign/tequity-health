About the company

Laptis works in behavioural health, where the path from referral to first appointment involves many people, many forms and a lot of follow-up.

The challenge

  1. 01
    Many referral sourcesReferrals arrive from different people and places, and the team needs to know who submitted each one.
  2. 02
    Forms that get finishedIntake depends on forms being completed on time, without staff chasing each one by hand.
  3. 03
    A shared team viewStaff need one place to see what is waiting and what happens next.

What Tequity built

Referrals and submitted-by trackingIntake
  • Referral capture with the submitting party recorded
  • Status for every referral from arrival to review
Forms and remindersFollow-up
  • Digital intake forms
  • Automated reminders for incomplete forms
CRM workflowsOperations
  • Queues and views for the intake team
  • Activity history on each referral

Process and approach

We mapped the intake process with the people who run it before building, so the CRM reflects how referrals actually move rather than a generic sales pipeline.
