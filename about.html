Our story

Why we built a healthcare practice.

Tequity started as an applied AI and product studio for founders. Over time, a growing share of our work moved into healthcare: care coordination, medical interpretation, patient outreach, clinical documentation, intake and referrals.

Those products taught us that healthcare software rarely fails because of the code. It fails at the edges: the workflow nobody mapped, the integration that took three times longer than planned, the AI feature with no answer for what happens when it is wrong. Tequity Health exists to bring that experience to every engagement from day one.

Delivery principles

How we make decisions on your product.

01
Workflow before features

We understand who does what, and where it breaks, before we decide what to build.

02
Humans own clinical decisions

We build AI that supports people and hands off clearly. Clinical logic stays with your clinical team.

03
Security is agreed, not assumed

Where PHI is involved, infrastructure, access and responsibilities are settled before development begins.

04
Honest about dependencies

EHR access, validation and partner timelines are named up front, not discovered in month three.

05
Say what we built

We describe our work precisely and leave clinical outcomes to the evidence our clients hold.

06
Leave you stronger

Documentation, knowledge transfer and code you own, so you are never locked in.
